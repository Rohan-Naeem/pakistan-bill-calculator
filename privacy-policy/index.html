Privacy Policy

This site does not require an account and does not ask for your reference number, consumer number, CNIC, address or payment details. Calculator inputs are processed in your own browser and are not sent to a server by this site's own code.

If advertising is added later (see the AdSense-ready placeholders), the advertising provider may use cookies under its own policy. That policy should be linked here before ads are enabled. No personal bill data is collected, sold or shared by this site.

External links to official company portals leave this site. Their privacy policies apply once you leave.

Pakistan Bill Calculator is an independent estimator. It is not an official website of LESCO, FESCO, GEPCO, IESCO, MEPCO, PESCO, HESCO, SEPCO, QESCO, TESCO, K-Electric, SNGPL, SSGC, WASA or KWSC. Results are estimates only.
