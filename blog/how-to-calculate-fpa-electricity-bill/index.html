How to Calculate FPA in Electricity Bill

FPA stands for Fuel Price Adjustment. It reflects the difference between the reference fuel cost assumed in the tariff and the actual fuel cost, as verified under the approved mechanism. That is why FPA can go up or down, and why it appears as its own line rather than being hidden inside the unit rate.

How FPA is calculated in words

FPA is normally applied per unit: your units multiplied by the approved FPA rate for that month. The rate depends on the month's fuel costs and on NEPRA's verification, so last month's FPA rate is not a safe guide to this month's.

Why FPA confuses people

FPA is often blamed for a high bill when the real cause is higher units. Check units first: if units are normal and FPA is unusually large, then look at the FPA rate and month on the bill before disputing anything else.
