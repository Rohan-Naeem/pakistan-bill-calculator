Protected vs Non-Protected Electricity Consumers

Protected and non-protected are domestic consumer categories that use different slab structures. Two homes with the same units can therefore pay different amounts, purely because of their category. That is why every calculator on this site asks for protected status before calculating.

What protected usually means

Protected status usually depends on low average consumption over previous months, under the current tariff rules. It is not a choice you tick on a form to get a cheaper bill; it follows your consumption history and the company's records.

How to check your category

Look at a recent bill: the category is printed near your consumer details. If it says protected and you expected non-protected (or the reverse), ask the company to confirm the basis, with your previous bills as evidence, rather than guessing in the calculator.
