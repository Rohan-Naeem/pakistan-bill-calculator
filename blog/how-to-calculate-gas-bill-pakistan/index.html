How to Calculate Gas Bill in Pakistan

Calculating a gas bill in Pakistan starts with one question: which company? SNGPL serves the north, SSGC serves the south, and both use HM3 slabs, fixed charges and GST, but with their own tariff schedules. Use your own company's calculator, not a generic gas calculator.

The method in words

Split HM3 across slabs, add the fixed charge for your category and protected status, then add GST and any meter rent on the tariff. Compare the result with the official bill, checking the billing period first — many domestic gas bills cover two months.

SNGPL vs SSGC

SNGPL consumers use an 11-digit consumer number for the official portal; SSGC consumers use a 10-digit customer number. Keep that number saved separately, because without it neither the duplicate bill nor most bank-app payments can be started.
