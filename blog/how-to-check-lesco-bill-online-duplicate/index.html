How to Check LESCO Bill Online and Download Duplicate

Checking a LESCO bill online means using the official PITC portal for LESCO, not a third-party page that asks for personal details. You need the 14-digit reference number from an old bill — that number is not your meter number.

Step by step

Open the official PITC portal for LESCO, enter the 14-digit reference number with no spaces or dashes, and submit. The current bill opens, showing units, readings, due date and total. From there, use your browser's print option and choose Save as PDF for a duplicate copy.

Safety rules

A genuine bill-check page never asks for a CNIC, password or card number just to show a bill. If a page asks for those, leave it. A printed duplicate from the official portal is normally accepted for payment at banks and authorised payment points.
