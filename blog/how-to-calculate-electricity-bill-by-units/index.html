Calculate Electricity Bill by Units in Pakistan

Calculating an electricity bill by units means charging each block of units at its own slab rate. The first block is charged at the lowest rate, the next block at the next rate, and so on. Only the units inside a block get that block's rate.

A worked method in words

Take your total units, fill the first slab, then the second, then the third, until all units are placed. Multiply each filled block by its rate and add the results. Then add fixed charges and taxes as separate lines. That total is your estimated bill.

Why the highest rate does not apply to all units

A common fear is that crossing into a higher slab reprices all your units. It does not: slabs are progressive, so only the units above the threshold are charged at the higher rate. The jump still matters, but it is smaller than the fear suggests.
