Water Bill Guide: WASA Lahore and KWSC Karachi

Water bills work differently from electricity and gas bills, because many connections are category-based rather than metered per gallon. That means using a little less water does not always change the bill, and the category on the bill matters more than a small change in usage.

WASA Lahore

WASA Lahore bills are often category-based, with a sewerage charge added. Check the category and the arrears line first if a total looks wrong, and use the official WASA portal with your account number for the duplicate bill.

KWSC Karachi

KWSC Karachi uses a consumer number that mixes letters and digits, typed exactly as printed with no spaces. Its online 'Get Your Bill' service shows the full bill, including arrears and payment history, which you can download or print.
