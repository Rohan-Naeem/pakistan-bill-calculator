Sub-Meter Electricity Bill: How to Calculate It

A sub-meter measures one part of a larger connection — a portion, a shop inside a building, or a rented floor. Its bill is calculated from its own reading difference, present minus previous, just like a main meter. The dispute is rarely the arithmetic; it is how the slabs and fixed charges are shared.

The fair method

Calculate the full main-meter bill first, using the main meter's units and slabs. Then agree, in writing, how that total is shared: by sub-meter units, by agreed shares, or by a fixed arrangement. Calculating two independent slab bills from one meter double-counts the low slabs.

Keep records

Both parties should keep dated sub-meter photos and a simple monthly log. With that log, a disagreement becomes a one-line check instead of a months-long argument.
