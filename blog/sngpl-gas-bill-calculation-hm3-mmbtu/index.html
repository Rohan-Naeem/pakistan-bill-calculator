SNGPL Gas Bill: HM3, MMBTU and GCV Explained

Gas bills confuse people because they use units most people never meet elsewhere: HM3, MMBTU and GCV. HM3 is the billing unit on most domestic gas bills. MMBTU is an energy unit. GCV, or gross calorific value, links gas volume to energy, and it varies, so the conversion on your own bill is the one to use.

How to calculate HM3 in a gas bill

Take the HM3 consumption for the billing period, split it across the gas slabs in order, add the fixed charge for your category, then add GST. If your bill is bi-monthly, enter the full two-month HM3, not half of it.

Why winter gas bills jump

Winter heating and longer geyser use raise HM3 sharply, and higher HM3 falls into higher slabs. A winter bill two or three times a summer bill is often a consumption story first, and only secondarily a rate story.
