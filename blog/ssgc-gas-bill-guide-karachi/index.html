SSGC Gas Bill Guide for Karachi and Sindh

SSGC serves Sindh and Balochistan, including Karachi, the largest gas-consuming city in Pakistan. Its domestic consumers use a 10-digit customer number on the official SSGC portal, and its bills follow the same HM3 slab logic as SNGPL, with SSGC's own tariff.

Checking and downloading an SSGC bill

Open the official SSGC view-bill portal, enter the 10-digit customer number exactly as printed, and submit. The bill opens with consumption, billing period, due date and total, and can be saved as a PDF from the browser's print option.

Winter and summer differences

Karachi gas use is steadier than in colder cities, but winter still raises geyser use. In Quetta, which SSGC also serves, winter heating can dominate the bill, so a Quetta winter bill should be compared with the previous winter, not with summer.
