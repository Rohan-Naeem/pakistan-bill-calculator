How to Calculate Bill from Watts and Appliances

Calculating a bill from watts is useful before you buy an appliance or when a bill jumps and you want to find the cause. List every major appliance, its watts (from its label), and its daily hours, then convert each to monthly kWh.

The steps

For each appliance: watts × hours per day × days in the month ÷ 1,000 = monthly kWh. Add all appliances together for total units, then apply the slab method from the main calculation guide. The result is an estimate, not a substitute for the meter.

What the watts method cannot do

It cannot capture standby power precisely, nor a faulty appliance that draws more than its label says. If the watts estimate is far below the meter units, check heaters, geysers, motors and old fridges first.
