How to Calculate Electricity Bill in Pakistan

This guide explains how to calculate an electricity bill in Pakistan from start to finish, without assuming you already know what a slab or an FPA is. It is the article to read first if every other guide jumps straight to numbers.

How the calculation works

Start with units in kWh, split them across slabs in order, add the fixed charge, then add adjustments and taxes. That order never changes, even when the rates change. If you skip the slab split and multiply all units by one rate, your answer will be wrong in almost every real case.

Common mistakes

The most common mistakes are entering the meter reading instead of the units, entering a two-month figure as one month, and choosing the wrong category. Fix those three first, and most estimates suddenly start matching real bills.
