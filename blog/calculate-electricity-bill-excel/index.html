Calculate Electricity Bill in Excel

Excel is a good place to check an electricity bill, because a slab table makes the calculation visible row by row. The method is the same as this site's calculator, but you build it yourself, so you can see every block.

Building the slab table

Make columns for slab, units in that slab, and rate. Fill units into each slab from your total, multiply each row, add the rows, then add fixed charges and taxes as separate rows at the bottom. That bottom total is your estimated bill.

The mistake to avoid

Never multiply all units by the highest slab rate. That error overstates the bill and is the reason many Excel estimates look 'wrong' compared with the official bill. If your Excel total and the calculator total differ, compare row by row until you find the slab that differs.
