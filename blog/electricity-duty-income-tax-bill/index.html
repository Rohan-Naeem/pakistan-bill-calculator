Electricity Duty and Income Tax on Your Bill

Three tax-like lines often appear on an electricity bill, and they are not interchangeable. Duty, GST and income-tax-related charges each follow different rules, which is why a bill can look heavily taxed even when the unit rate itself has not changed.

Electricity duty

Duty is charged as a percentage on the relevant part of the bill. Its rate and base are set by the applicable rules for that consumer and area, so a duty figure from another province or another category is not a reliable check.

Income tax and further tax

Income-tax-related lines on electricity bills depend on factors such as consumer category and bill amount or history, under the applicable tax rules. Because those rules are conditional, this site notes them rather than guessing a figure for every consumer.
