AC Bill and Inverter AC Bill Explained

There is no separate AC tariff. An air conditioner simply adds kWh to your normal bill, and those extra kWh fall into higher slabs if your total units are already high. That slab effect is why one AC can seem to 'double' a bill in summer.

Inverter vs non-inverter

An inverter AC adjusts its compressor speed instead of switching fully on and off, so for the same cooling it usually uses fewer kWh than an old non-inverter AC. The saving depends on room size, insulation, thermostat setting and hours used — not on the word 'inverter' alone.

Saving without buying a new AC

Setting the thermostat a couple of degrees higher and using a fan with the AC usually saves more units than switching off lights. Clean filters, closed doors and sensible hours matter more than any single gadget claim.
