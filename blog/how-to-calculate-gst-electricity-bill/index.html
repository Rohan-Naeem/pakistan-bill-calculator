How to Calculate GST on Electricity Bill

GST on an electricity bill is a tax applied to the relevant base amount on the bill, at the rate in force for that bill. It is shown as a separate line, not folded into the slab rate, so you can see exactly how much of the total is tax.

GST, duty and income tax are different lines

Electricity duty is a separate provincial-style charge, GST is a federal tax, and income-tax-related lines (where they apply) depend on consumer type and bill history. Do not add them together as one 'tax' percentage, because each has its own base.

Checking the tax lines

To check GST, multiply the correct base by the GST rate shown on the tariff for that month. If the result does not match the bill, check whether the base includes fixed charges and adjustments, or only the energy charge — that base is the usual source of a mismatch.
