Electricity Bill from Meter Reading: A Simple Guide

Your meter reading is the only honest starting point for an electricity bill. The bill's units are simply the present reading minus the previous reading, both printed on the bill. If you can read those two numbers, you can calculate the bill.

Reading the meter yourself

Take a dated photo of the meter on reading day, and note the reading in your phone. Compare it with the readings printed on the bill when it arrives. If they differ, the photo is your evidence for a correction.

When the reading is estimated

If the meter could not be read, the company may bill on an estimated or average basis under its rules. Compare that estimate with your normal months, keep your own readings, and ask for a correction if the estimate is clearly out of line.
