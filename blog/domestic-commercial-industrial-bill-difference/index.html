Domestic, Commercial and Industrial Bills Compared

Category is the single biggest hidden variable in an electricity bill. A domestic, commercial and industrial connection with the same units will not produce the same bill, because each category has its own slab structure and fixed charges.

Domestic

Domestic bills use the protected / non-protected slab system, with fixed charges for homes. They are designed for household use, not for running a shop or a workshop from the same connection.

Commercial and industrial

Commercial and industrial bills use different rates and, for larger connections, demand-related concepts such as sanctioned load and MDI. Running commercial load on a domestic connection is a common cause of disputes, back-billing and category correction.
