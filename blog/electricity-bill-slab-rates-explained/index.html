Electricity Bill Slab Rates Explained

Slab rates are progressive: the first units are cheapest, and each next block is charged at a higher rate. That design means a bill does not rise in a straight line with units — it rises faster once you cross into higher slabs.

Protected and non-protected slabs

Domestic protected and non-protected consumers have different slab structures, which is why category matters as much as units. Commercial and industrial consumers have their own structures again, so a slab table for one category must never be applied to another.

Using slabs to save money

Because only units above a threshold get the higher rate, saving even a few units near a threshold can save more than saving the same units at the bottom of the scale. That is why mid-month unit checks are more useful than end-of-month surprises.
