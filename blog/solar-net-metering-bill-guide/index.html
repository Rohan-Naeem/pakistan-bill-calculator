Solar Net Metering Bill Guide for Pakistan

Net metering needs two numbers, not one: imported units from the grid and exported units from your solar system. A bi-directional meter records both, and the bill credits exports under the approved terms, then adds fixed charges and taxes.

Import and export are not the same rate

Export credit is applied at the official export rate in your approved net-metering agreement, which is not necessarily equal to the import rate. Subtracting exports from imports and multiplying by one rate therefore gives the wrong answer.

Why a solar bill is rarely zero

Fixed charges, taxes and any imported units still appear, and export credit may be banked rather than paid out. Check the banking period in your agreement before assuming a large summer surplus will still be there in winter.
