Duplicate Bill Download Guide for Pakistan

A duplicate bill is simply a copy of the official bill, printed or saved as a PDF from the official portal. It is normally accepted for payment at banks and authorised payment points, provided the reference number, billing month and due date are visible.

Electricity duplicate bills

For DISCOs, use the PITC portal with the 14-digit reference number. For K-Electric, use ke.com.pk with the 13-digit account number. In both cases, use the browser's print option and choose Save as PDF.

Gas and water duplicate bills

For SNGPL, use the official SNGPL portal with the 11-digit consumer number; for SSGC, use viewbill.ssgc.com.pk with the 10-digit customer number. For WASA Lahore and KWSC Karachi, use their official portals with the account/consumer number from an old bill. Never enter card details just to view a bill.
