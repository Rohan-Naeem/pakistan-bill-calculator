How to use this SSGC calculator — step by step

This guidance is specific to Sui Southern Gas Company (Sindh and Balochistan). Follow it in order, and the estimate will match the way SSGC itself builds a bill.

  1. Find your consumption in HM3 on your last SSGC bill. If the bill shows MMBTU, use the conversion printed on the bill before entering a figure.
  2. Check the billing period. Many domestic gas bills cover two months. Enter the full period's HM3, not half of it.
  3. Choose your category and protected status exactly as printed on your bill.
  4. Press Calculate and read the slab breakdown, fixed charge and GST line by line.
  5. Compare with the official bill using your 10-digit customer number on the official SSGC portal, and keep the PDF copy for payment.

Tip for SSGC users: SSGC consumers should keep the 10-digit customer number from an old bill saved separately. Without it, neither the online duplicate bill nor most bank-app payments can be started.

How a SSGC bill is calculated

A gas bill in Sindh and Balochistan starts with your consumption in HM3, split across slabs in the same way as an electricity bill: the first HM3 fall in the lowest slab and later HM3 in higher slabs. Only the HM3 in each slab are charged at that slab's rate. After the slab cost, a fixed charge is added, which differs by category and by protected or non-protected status for domestic consumers.

GST and any meter rent or other charges shown on the official tariff are then added. If your bill is bi-monthly, remember that the consumption figure covers two months, so the slab effect is stronger than it would be for the same monthly average split across two separate bills. That is a common source of confusion when a winter gas bill arrives.

Protected status matters a great deal for gas. It is not a choice you make on the bill; it follows the tariff rules for domestic consumers. If you enter the wrong protected status here, the estimate will be wrong even if the HM3 figure is exact, so check the category printed on a recent bill first.

How to download a duplicate bill

Once the bill opens on the official portal, use your browser's print option and choose Save as PDF, or print it directly. On a phone, use Share, then Print. Keep the PDF for payment and for your records, and check that the reference number, billing month and due date are all visible on the copy.

Complaints and corrections

If a bill looks wrong, work through it in this order: units and readings, category, billing period, arrears, then taxes and adjustments. Most disputes are settled at one of those steps without a formal complaint, because the cause is usually a reading, a category or an arrears figure, not a hidden charge.

If the bill is still wrong after that check, raise a complaint through the official SSGC complaint channel or your nearest SSGC office, with your reference or consumer number, the bill month and a dated photo of your meter if the reading is disputed. Helpline numbers are not printed on this page on purpose: they change, and a wrong number here would be worse than no number. Use the contact page on the official SSGC website linked above for the current number.

Keep a copy of any complaint reference you are given, and keep the bill and the meter photo together until the correction appears on a later bill. If a correction is promised, check the next bill — do not assume it has been applied.

Common mistakes that make an estimate wrong

  • Entering the meter reading instead of the units used that month. Units are present reading minus previous reading.
  • Entering a two-month figure as if it were one month, especially for gas.
  • Choosing the wrong category, or guessing protected status instead of checking the bill.
  • Forgetting arrears or a late surcharge that are already on the official bill.
  • Comparing rupees with last month instead of comparing units, which hides whether usage or rates changed.

A good habit is to write down your units every month, next to the bill total. After three months you will see your own pattern, and a sudden jump will stand out immediately, whether it is usage, a slab change or an error.

Frequently asked questions about SSGC

How do I use the SSGC gas bill calculator?

Enter your gas consumption in HM3, choose your category, and choose protected or non-protected for a domestic connection. Press Calculate to see the slab breakdown, fixed charges and taxes. If your bill shows MMBTU instead of HM3, use the conversion shown on the bill before entering a figure.

Is the SSGC gas bill calculator free?

Yes, it is free and needs no account, consumer number or personal detail. You only enter a consumption figure to get an estimate.

What is the difference between protected and non-protected gas consumers?

Gas domestic consumers are divided into protected and non-protected categories under the OGRA tariff, broadly based on consumption levels. The slab rates and fixed charges differ between the two, so choosing the wrong one gives a wrong estimate even if the HM3 figure is right.

Why is my SSGC gas bill so high in winter?

Winter heating and longer geyser use raise HM3 sharply, and the slab system means higher consumption is charged at higher slab rates. A winter bill that is two or three times a summer bill is often a consumption story first, and a rate story second.

Why do I sometimes get no SSGC bill in a month?

Many domestic gas consumers are billed every two months. If no bill appears in a given month, it may simply be an off-cycle month, not a missing bill. Check the billing period on the last bill you received.

Does this calculator show my actual SSGC bill?

No, it shows an estimate. The official bill also includes arrears, meter rent where applicable, GST and any adjustment for that billing period. Use the official bill for the amount to pay.

How do I check my SSGC bill online?

Use the official SSGC bill portal linked on this page and enter your consumer or customer number exactly as printed on an old bill. Do not enter card details or a password just to view a bill.

How do I download a duplicate SSGC gas bill?

After the bill opens on the official portal, use the print or download option in your browser and save it as a PDF. Keep the PDF for payment and for your own records.

What should I do if my SSGC gas bill looks wrong?

Check the consumption, category and billing period on the bill, then compare with the previous bill. If something does not match, contact SSGC through its official complaint channel with your consumer number and the bill month ready.
