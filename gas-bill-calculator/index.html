    How to calculate gas bill and sui gas bill in Pakistan

    To calculate a gas bill, start with consumption in HM3, split it across the gas slabs, add the fixed charge for your category, then add GST and any meter rent shown on the tariff. That is the gas bill calculation formula in Pakistan in words. Whether you searched for gas bill calculator or sui gas bill calculator, this hub is the right starting point: choose SNGPL for the north or SSGC for the south. Many people ask how to calculate HM3 in a gas bill, or how to calculate MMBTU in a gas bill: HM3 is the billing unit on most domestic gas bills, MMBTU is an energy unit, and GCV (gross calorific value) is the factor that links gas volume to energy. Use the conversion printed on your own bill, because GCV varies.

    Sui gas bill calculation means the same calculation for SNGPL in the north and SSGC in the south — Sui Northern and Sui Southern are the two companies, not two different formulas. Remember also that many domestic gas bills cover two months, so enter the full billing-period consumption, not half of it.
