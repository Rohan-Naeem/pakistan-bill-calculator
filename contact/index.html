Contact

Use this page to report a wrong rate, a broken calculator, a wrong official link, or a page that needs updating. The most useful report includes: the company, the page URL, the units and category you entered, and the official source (NEPRA / OGRA notification or company tariff page) that shows the correct rate.

Contact details for the site owner should be added here before launch — do not publish a personal phone number or email on this page unless you intend it to be public. For bill-specific problems (a wrong reading, a disputed amount, a new connection), contact your company directly through its official channel, not through this site, because this site cannot access your account.
