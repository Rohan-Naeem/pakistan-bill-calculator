    How to calculate electricity bill in Pakistan

    To calculate an electricity bill, start with units in kWh, split them across slabs, add the fixed charge, then add Fuel Price Adjustment, quarterly adjustment, electricity duty, GST and the TV fee. That is the electricity bill calculation formula in Pakistan in words — slabs first, then charges, then taxes — and it applies whether the company is LESCO, FESCO, GEPCO, IESCO, MEPCO, PESCO, HESCO, SEPCO, QESCO, TESCO or K-Electric. WAPDA bill calculation questions usually mean the same thing, because the DISCOs were historically part of WAPDA's distribution system; today you should use your own DISCO's calculator, not a generic WAPDA one.

    Two industrial terms often cause confusion. MDI, or maximum demand indicator, relates to the highest load recorded for some industrial and large commercial connections, and power factor relates to how efficiently that load uses electricity. Neither is a normal domestic bill line, so if they appear on a domestic bill, check the category first. You can also calculate an electricity bill in Excel using the slab-table method explained on the LESCO page, which is the same method this site's calculator uses.
