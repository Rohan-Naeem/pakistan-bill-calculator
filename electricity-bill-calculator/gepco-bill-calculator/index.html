How to use this GEPCO calculator — step by step

This guidance is specific to Gujranwala Electric Power Company (Gujranwala region). Follow it in order, and the estimate will match the way GEPCO itself builds a bill.

  1. Find your units. Use the units printed on your last GEPCO bill, or subtract the previous meter reading from the present reading. Do not enter the meter reading itself — enter the difference.
  2. Choose your category. Domestic, commercial or industrial, exactly as printed on your bill. A wrong category gives a wrong estimate even if the units are right.
  3. Choose protected or non-protected (domestic only). If you are not sure, check a recent bill: the category is printed near your consumer details.
  4. Leave the custom-rate field blank to use the official tariff from tariffs.json. Use it only if you want an estimate from a rate on your own bill — that result is labelled user-entered, not official.
  5. Press Calculate. Read the breakdown table slab by slab, then the fixed charges, adjustments and taxes. The total at the top is the estimated bill.
  6. Use Print, Save as PDF, Copy or Share to keep the result. Then compare it with your official GEPCO bill, using your 14-digit reference number on the official portal. Compare units first, then category, then arrears.

Tip for GEPCO users: Sialkot and Gujrat homes that run motors, welders or small workshop equipment on a domestic connection should check their sanctioned load and category first, because the wrong category changes the whole bill.

How a GEPCO bill is calculated

An electricity bill in Gujranwala region is not a single price multiplied by your units. It is built in layers. First, your units are split across slabs, with the first units in the lowest slab and each next block in a higher slab. Only the units in a slab are charged at that slab's rate, which is why two bills with a small difference in units can still have a noticeable difference in rupees if the extra units fall in a higher slab.

Second, fixed charges are added. These do not depend on how many units you used that month, and they differ by category. Third, adjustments such as Fuel Price Adjustment and quarterly adjustment are added where they apply. Finally, taxes and fees, including electricity duty, GST and the TV fee, are applied to the relevant parts of the bill. The calculator on this page follows that same order, so the breakdown table reads like the bill itself: slabs first, then fixed charges, then adjustments, then taxes.

Protected and non-protected domestic consumers are treated separately because their slab structures differ. If you are not sure which one you are, look at a recent bill: the category is normally printed near the consumer details. Do not guess, because choosing the wrong category is the most common reason an estimate does not match a real bill.

How to check your GEPCO bill online

  1. Find the 14-digit reference number on any old GEPCO bill. It is not the meter number.
  2. Open the official PITC portal for GEPCO.
  3. Enter the reference number with no spaces or dashes and submit.
  4. Check the units, readings, due date and total on the bill that opens.

How to download a duplicate bill

Once the bill opens on the official portal, use your browser's print option and choose Save as PDF, or print it directly. On a phone, use Share, then Print. Keep the PDF for payment and for your records, and check that the reference number, billing month and due date are all visible on the copy.

Complaints and corrections

If a bill looks wrong, work through it in this order: units and readings, category, billing period, arrears, then taxes and adjustments. Most disputes are settled at one of those steps without a formal complaint, because the cause is usually a reading, a category or an arrears figure, not a hidden charge.

If the bill is still wrong after that check, raise a complaint through the official GEPCO complaint channel or your nearest GEPCO office, with your reference or consumer number, the bill month and a dated photo of your meter if the reading is disputed. Helpline numbers are not printed on this page on purpose: they change, and a wrong number here would be worse than no number. Use the contact page on the official GEPCO website linked above for the current number.

Keep a copy of any complaint reference you are given, and keep the bill and the meter photo together until the correction appears on a later bill. If a correction is promised, check the next bill — do not assume it has been applied.

Common mistakes that make an estimate wrong

  • Entering the meter reading instead of the units used that month. Units are present reading minus previous reading.
  • Entering a two-month figure as if it were one month, especially for gas.
  • Choosing the wrong category, or guessing protected status instead of checking the bill.
  • Forgetting arrears or a late surcharge that are already on the official bill.
  • Comparing rupees with last month instead of comparing units, which hides whether usage or rates changed.

A good habit is to write down your units every month, next to the bill total. After three months you will see your own pattern, and a sudden jump will stand out immediately, whether it is usage, a slab change or an error.

Frequently asked questions about GEPCO

How do I use the GEPCO bill calculator?

Enter the kWh from your last bill or your meter reading difference, choose domestic or commercial, and choose protected or non-protected if you are a domestic consumer. Press Calculate. The calculator then shows the slab breakdown, fixed charges and taxes line by line, so you can see exactly which part of the bill is largest.

Is the GEPCO bill calculator free?

Yes. Using the calculator on this page is free, and you do not need to create an account or enter your reference number, CNIC or any personal detail to get an estimate.

What is a protected consumer in GEPCO?

Protected status is a domestic consumer category that usually depends on low average consumption over previous months. Protected consumers are charged under a different slab structure from non-protected consumers, which is why the calculator asks you to choose one. Confirm the current rule from the official GEPCO tariff before relying on a protected estimate.

Why is my GEPCO bill higher than my neighbour's?

Two homes can pay very different amounts even in the same street, because electricity is charged in slabs. Once your units cross into a higher slab, only the extra units are charged at the higher rate, but the total still rises fast. Compare units first, then compare categories, then compare the tax and adjustment lines.

What are FPA and quarterly adjustment on a GEPCO bill?

FPA stands for Fuel Price Adjustment and quarterly adjustment is a separate periodic adjustment. Both reflect changes in generation and system costs that are passed through under the approved tariff mechanism. They are shown as separate lines on the bill, and this calculator shows them as separate lines too, once the official rates are filled in.

Does this calculator show my actual GEPCO bill?

No. It shows an estimate based on the units and category you enter. Your actual bill also depends on the official meter reading, arrears, late surcharge, meter rent where applicable, and any correction or adjustment for that month. Always check the official bill for the amount you must pay.

How can I check my GEPCO bill online?

Use the official PITC portal for GEPCO and enter your 14-digit reference number from an old bill. The portal shows the current bill, which you can print or save as a PDF. This site does not fetch your personal bill data and never asks for your reference number.

How do I get a duplicate GEPCO bill?

Open your bill on the official portal, then use your browser's print option and choose Save as PDF, or print it directly. A printed duplicate from the official portal is normally accepted for payment at banks and other authorised payment points.

What should I do if my GEPCO bill looks wrong?

First compare the present and previous readings on the bill with your own meter, and check the units and category. If the reading is wrong, keep a dated photo of the meter and raise a complaint through the official GEPCO complaint channel or your nearest office, with the reference number and the bill month ready.
