Faisalabad Electricity Bill Calculator 2026 – FESCO

Faisalabad combines city homes with textile and small-industry load, so checking whether a connection is domestic or commercial matters more here than in a purely residential city. This page is the Faisalabad version of the FESCO calculator, so you do not have to guess which company serves your city. It uses the same official tariff data as the main FESCO page, the same slab breakdown, and the same disclaimer: this is an estimate, not an official bill.

FESCO calculator

Base rates verified: domestic, commercial (up to 5 kW) and industrial (B1 up to 25 kW) base rates are from NEPRA S.R.O. 279(I)/2026, as published in the IESCO Tariff Guide (checked 2026-11-01). The total includes duty, GST and TV fee where shown. Blank means the total excludes that month’s FPA and quarterly adjustment, which change monthly and are never hardcoded here — enter the rates from your bill in the optional FPA and quarterly adjustment fields below to include them. How to use the Faisalabad calculator — step by step

  1. Find your units: present meter reading minus previous reading, or the units printed on your last FESCO bill.
  2. Choose your category exactly as printed on the bill, and protected or non-protected for a domestic connection.
  3. Press Calculate, then read the slab breakdown before looking only at the total.
  4. Print, save as PDF, copy or share the result, then compare it with the official FESCO bill — units first, then category, then arrears.

Open the main FESCO Bill Calculator page for the full tariff table, FAQs and complaint guidance. For gas in Faisalabad, use SNGPL if you are in the north or SSGC if you are in the south; for water, use WASA Lahore in Lahore or KWSC in Karachi.

Frequently asked questions — Faisalabad

Which company supplies electricity in Faisalabad?

Faisalabad is supplied by Faisalabad Electric Supply Company (FESCO). If the top of your old bill shows a different company name, use that company's calculator instead — the company follows your connection, not just your city.

How do I calculate my Faisalabad electricity bill?

Enter your units in kWh, choose domestic, commercial or industrial, and choose protected or non-protected for a domestic connection, then press Calculate. The calculator splits your units across slabs, adds fixed charges and taxes, and shows the breakdown.

How do I check my Faisalabad bill online?

Use the official bill portal for FESCO linked on this page, with the number from an old bill. For most DISCOs that is a 14-digit reference number; for K-Electric in Karachi it is a 13-digit account number.
