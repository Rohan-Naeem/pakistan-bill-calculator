How to use the Bahawalpur calculator — step by step

  1. Find your units: present meter reading minus previous reading, or the units printed on your last MEPCO bill.
  2. Choose your category exactly as printed on the bill, and protected or non-protected for a domestic connection.
  3. Press Calculate, then read the slab breakdown before looking only at the total.
  4. Print, save as PDF, copy or share the result, then compare it with the official MEPCO bill — units first, then category, then arrears.

Frequently asked questions — Bahawalpur

Which company supplies electricity in Bahawalpur?

Bahawalpur is supplied by Multan Electric Power Company (MEPCO). If the top of your old bill shows a different company name, use that company's calculator instead — the company follows your connection, not just your city.

How do I calculate my Bahawalpur electricity bill?

Enter your units in kWh, choose domestic, commercial or industrial, and choose protected or non-protected for a domestic connection, then press Calculate. The calculator splits your units across slabs, adds fixed charges and taxes, and shows the breakdown.

How do I check my Bahawalpur bill online?

Use the official bill portal for MEPCO linked on this page, with the number from an old bill. For most DISCOs that is a 14-digit reference number; for K-Electric in Karachi it is a 13-digit account number.
