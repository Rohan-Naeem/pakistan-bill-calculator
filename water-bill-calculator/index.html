    How a water bill is calculated in Pakistan

    To calculate a water bill, start with your category. Many WASA and KWSC connections are category-based, so the monthly charge, sewerage charge and any arrears matter more than a small change in gallons used. Where a connection is metered, consumption is charged under the applicable rate and sewerage is added separately. In both cases, arrears from earlier months are added to the current bill, which is the most common reason a water bill suddenly looks double.
