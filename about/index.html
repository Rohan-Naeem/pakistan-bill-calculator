About Pakistan Bill Calculator

Pakistan Bill Calculator is an independent, free estimator for electricity, gas and water bills in Pakistan. It was built because most bill calculators online show a single number with no working, no source and no way to check whether the rate is current.

This site does the opposite: it shows slabs, fixed charges, adjustments and taxes line by line, it stores all rates in one open file (/data/tariffs.json) that anyone can inspect, and it refuses to invent a total while a rate is still marked VERIFY_RATE. That honesty is deliberate. A wrong-looking total with a confident design is worse than a clearly labelled pending rate.

We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting for any distribution company, regulator or government body. Company names are used only to identify whose tariff a calculator estimates.

Pakistan Bill Calculator is an independent estimator. It is not an official website of LESCO, FESCO, GEPCO, IESCO, MEPCO, PESCO, HESCO, SEPCO, QESCO, TESCO, K-Electric, SNGPL, SSGC, WASA or KWSC. Results are estimates only.
